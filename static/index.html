      QUICK GUIDE

      From a photo to a result
      you can inspect.

      Upload the first photo. ScanPilot straightens the page, reads its text, and marks uncertain regions. You can also mark any missed areas in the recovery panel.

      Add a second photo after moving the obstruction. Include surrounding text and the document number so the photos can be matched. Click an OCR line or page region to see its source. Review the result, then download text, a PDF, or an evidence report.

      Quality thresholds are preliminary; colored pages, glare, large tables, and busy backgrounds can cause errors. White covers and fully missing text may go undetected. Matching does not prove document identity. OCR scores are not calibrated. Use up to two photos of one flat page; missing characters are not guessed.
